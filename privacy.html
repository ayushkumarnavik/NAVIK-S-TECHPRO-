Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how NAVIK'S TECHPRO handles information submitted by visitors and customers when they contact us or request technology-related services.

Before publishing this policy, the website owner should confirm that the practices described below match the website's actual data collection and storage systems.

1. Information We May Collect

Depending on the services requested, information may include:

  • Name and contact details.
  • Email address and telephone number.
  • City and preferred contact method.
  • Project title, requirements, and technical specifications.
  • Budget, deadlines, and delivery preferences.
  • Images, documents, diagrams, and other project files.
  • Messages exchanged about a project or quotation.

2. How Information May Be Used

Information may be used to:

  • Understand project requirements.
  • Prepare quotations and estimated project costs.
  • Communicate about orders, changes, and delivery.
  • Provide technical consultation and customer support.
  • Maintain business records and prevent misuse.
  • Meet applicable legal obligations.

Personal information should not be used for unrelated purposes without an appropriate lawful basis.

3. File Uploads

Customers may be invited to submit project-related images and documents. Do not upload passwords, identity documents, payment credentials, or unrelated confidential information.

A working upload system should validate file types and sizes, scan files where appropriate, and restrict access to authorised people. Files should not be stored in a publicly accessible folder.

4. Storage and Security

Reasonable technical and organisational measures should be used to protect customer information against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or loss.

No online system can guarantee absolute security. A real deployment should use HTTPS, secure authentication, server-side access controls, and protected database access.

5. Sharing of Information

Information should only be shared when reasonably necessary to provide a requested service, operate trusted service providers, comply with applicable law, or protect legitimate rights and safety.

Customer information should not be sold to third parties. Any actual third-party sharing practices must be disclosed accurately before this policy is published.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Information should be retained only for as long as necessary for the stated purposes or applicable legal requirements. A functioning website should establish a process for handling appropriate requests to access, correct, or delete personal information.

7. Cookies and Analytics

This static page does not itself implement analytics or cookie tracking. If analytics, advertising tools, login sessions, or other tracking technologies are added, this policy should be updated to explain their use.

8. Children's Privacy

School and student project enquiries should be handled carefully. Where required, appropriate parent or guardian involvement and consent should be obtained before collecting or processing a child's personal information.
